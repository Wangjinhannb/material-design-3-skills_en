# Dialogs (`dialogs`)

## Purpose

Request confirmation, selection, or completion of a short task.

## Variants

- `basic`
- `fullscreen`

## States

- `default`
- `hovered`
- `focused`
- `pressed`
- `disabled`

## Token groups

`color`, `typography`, `shape`, `state`

## Accessibility

Move focus into the dialog when opened, restore it sensibly on close, and provide an accessible name or title.
