# Checkbox (`checkbox`)

## Purpose

Toggle independent choices within a form or group.

## Variants

- `unchecked`
- `checked`
- `indeterminate`

## States

- `unchecked`
- `checked`
- `indeterminate`
- `hovered`
- `focused`
- `pressed`
- `disabled`

## Token groups

`color`, `typography`, `shape`, `state`

## Accessibility

Expose checked and indeterminate states.
