# Bottom app bar (`bottom-app-bar`)

## Purpose

Place primary actions at the bottom of a mobile layout, optionally alongside a FAB.

## Variants

- `standard`

## States

- `default`
- `hovered`
- `focused`
- `pressed`
- `disabled`

## Token groups

`color`, `typography`, `shape`, `state`

## Accessibility

Every action requires a clear name. Avoid system gesture areas.
