# Bottom sheets (`bottom-sheets`)

## Purpose

Present supporting content or tasks from the bottom edge.

## Variants

- `standard`
- `modal`

## States

- `default`
- `hovered`
- `focused`
- `pressed`
- `disabled`

## Token groups

`color`, `typography`, `shape`, `state`

## Accessibility

Modal sheets manage focus and prevent background interaction while open.
