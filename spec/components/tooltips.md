# Tooltips (`tooltips`)

## Purpose

Explain unfamiliar controls or provide supplemental information.

## Variants

- `plain`
- `rich`

## States

- `hidden`
- `visible`

## Token groups

`color`, `typography`, `shape`, `state`

## Accessibility

Task-critical information must also be available without hover and from keyboard focus.
